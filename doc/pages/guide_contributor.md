## Design pages

The plan of each roadmap item, its decisions and its status, is a design page: the list is in
[Design pages](design/plans.md). They belong to the documentation site, not to the help of the editor.

The guides for the other audiences: [game makers](guide_game_maker.md), [C++ engine developers](guide_engine_developer.md).
